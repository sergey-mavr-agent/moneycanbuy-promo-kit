# Brief: moneycanbuy.fun 30 s promo

This is the exact text of the IMD swarm order. Paths are relative to this repository.

moneycanbuy.fun: make a 30-second promo for X, rendered entirely in CODE (no image, video or music generation models), flat bold motion graphics. The files in this repository are yours to use: read BRIEF.md (this same text), then watch reference/test-B.mp4 and reference/stills/: that is the style. Black background, our real site UI cards (ui/), big Quicksand Bold type (brand/fonts/), colors from brand/colors.md, venue logos from logos/. Clarity first: a viewer must get "post a dare, someone does it, gets paid" in one watch.

PRODUCT: a board of paid dares. Anyone posts a dare with a reward, anyone does it and posts proof, the IMD swarm judges, the doer claims the reward.

MUSIC: use the supplied input "music" (the full 30.000 s track) as the soundtrack, from 0.000 s, never cut, stretched or retimed. 108 BPM, beat 0.5556 s, bar 2.222 s. First drop 4.98 s, beats at 5.000 + n x 0.5556 s. Near-silent dip 20.56-21.07 s, second drop 21.07 s, the 808 stops 26.61 s, last hit 28.05 s. You may raise loudness to about -9 LUFS with a limiter (true peak -1 dBTP max) and add a sound-effects layer made in code under the music: whoosh on whips, a riser over 20.0-21.07 s, marker squeak, register beep, camera shutters, a stamp on every PAID, coins, a bright ring on the $YOLO coin. No voice.

OUR TOAD: when a costume appears it is a HUMAN wearing our toad as a plush mascot head, drawn exactly as toad-head/front.png and toad-head/three-quarter.png (face from brand/mark.png: heavy half-closed lids, flat smirk), with the gold chain and the gold "$" tag always visible. Never film it from behind, never a toad wearing a toad, never round googly eyes. Pepe masks are flat masks of the same face.

PEOPLE: flat bold figures in the toad-head style (thick dark outline, flat fills, simple face shapes, real limb motion). No real or famous people, no photos.

THE LOOP for every dare, same each time:
(a) CARD, 2 beats: the dare card in our site style (ui/card-*.png: dark rounded card, cover on the left, white title, reward in gold #F5C451 as "$40") fills the screen. On beat 3 it snaps into a fixed plate at the top of the frame and stays there until the dare ends.
(b) ACTION, from beat 3: the dare as kinetic graphics; for venue dares the venue logo slams in on beat 3.
(c) PAID, beat 4 to the end of beat 5: big gold "PAID $40" on a fixed plate in the lower third (it starts scaling in 3 frames before the beat, so it lands on the beat and stays 1.2 s), coins burst from it.

TRANSITIONS, only these, always on a beat and the same move for the same job: whip pan with motion blur (4-6 frames) into each new dare card; zoom punch-in (3-5 frames) from card to action; 1-2 frame white or gold flash on PAID hits and on the Claim tap; logo wipe (the venue logo scales through the frame). Forbidden: cuts to black, crossfades or slow dissolves, any frame frozen over 1 s before 28 s. Every shot moves: a slow camera drift on the background, motion on the people and props.

TEXT: every text block sits on a plate fixed to the screen grid, never attached to a moving object, never sliding. Each text block stays on screen at least 1.2 s. At most 6 words of big type per screen; the dare card and the end card are the only exceptions and carry exactly the words above. No text smaller than 60 px cap height in the vertical file. English only, spelled exactly as written in this text, nothing else, and never write that payment waits for anything.

REJECTION LIST, check every second of both files and redo anything that fails before delivery:
1. Drifting text: any text glued to a moving object or sliding across the frame.
2. Wrong toad: not drawn as toad-head/ and brand/mark.png, googly eyes, missing chain or "$" tag, a toad in a toad suit.
3. Black gaps: any fully black frame from 3.0 to 26.61 s.
4. Frozen frames: any picture held over 1 s before 28 s.
5. Misspellings: any word not exactly as given here.
6. Letterbox: bars, blur or an empty half in the wide file.
7. Unreadable text: under 1.2 s, under 60 px cap height, or low contrast.
Also: cuts off the beat grid by more than 1 frame, the music retimed, a real brand or face.
